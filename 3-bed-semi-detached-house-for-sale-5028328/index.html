About the property

Description property

Excellent three-bedroom semi-detached residence situated in the established and highly regarded Oranview development in Oranmore. No. 8 enjoys a convenient location within easy reach of Oranmore Village and all local amenities, offering an ideal balance of comfort, convenience and modern family living.

The property has been carefully maintained by its current owners and presents in excellent condition throughout. Accommodation on the ground floor comprises an entrance hall, a comfortable living room with an open fire, a spacious kitchen and dining area overlooking the rear garden, together with a utility room and guest WC. On the two upper floors, there are three bedrooms, including a principal bedroom with ensuite, and a main family bathroom.

Outside, the property benefits from a private rear garden providing an ideal space for outdoor dining, gardening or relaxing, while the front driveway offers off-street parking. The development itself is mature and well maintained, making it particularly appealing to families, first-time buyers and those seeking a convenient village setting.

Located just a short distance from schools, shops, cafés, sporting facilities and public transport links, No. 8 also enjoys easy access to Galway City, the M6 Motorway and the many attractions of Oranmore and its surrounding area.

Overall, No. 8 Oranview is a turnkey home in a highly desirable residential location, offering comfortable accommodation and excellent convenience for a wide range of purchasers.
